The Story

The long way here

I grew up on a farm in northern New South Wales, raised by my grandmother, never quite fitting the mould. Turns out that was the point.

The path since has not been a straight line, and I would not trade it for one. Teaching English in Shanghai. Building friendships in Nepal. Dancing in front of half a million people in Sri Lanka. More than a decade working across China, Southeast Asia, Central Asia and Hong Kong, usually as the only Westerner in the room.

What I learned along the way is simple: business runs on trust, and trust runs on genuine human connection. Not cold outreach. Not polished pitch decks. Sitting down with people, listening to them and solving their problems together.

I'm good with people, particularly across cultures. I connect quickly, build trust naturally, and tend to understand what matters to people beneath the surface. Whether I'm in Chiang Mai, Shanghai or Sydney, I can usually find common ground, bring the right people together, and turn those relationships into something meaningful.

What I Do

Doors open differently in every culture.
I know which way they swing.

For over two decades I've been building relationships across Asia, Australia and beyond. I specialise in the work that can't be outsourced: being the person who is already there, already trusted, already fluent in how things actually get done. These days I'm pairing that with AI. I've worked with LLMs daily since early 2023 and build my own tools with them, including a life dashboard and a personal agent I run every day.

Strategic Partnerships

Finding the right people, building real relationships with them, and turning those relationships into outcomes that last. From street level to boardroom, across every culture I've worked in.

Market Entry & Expansion

A deal done right in Bangkok is done very differently to one in Buenos Aires or Brisbane. I help organisations grow across emerging markets without the expensive lessons.

Cross-Cultural Bridge Building

I've navigated complex Chinese corporate environments as a trusted Western bridge and built networks that span continents. When two worlds need to understand each other, that's my room.

Speaking & Brand Representation

Stages, expos, delegations and dinners across Asia and beyond. I represent organisations the way I represent myself: warmly, directly and without a script.

What I Believe
“When I speak my truth,
I feel my power
and live in freedom.”

That's my personal creed, and it took me fifty years to earn it. I believe every person has a purpose, and mine is connection: helping people, organisations and cultures genuinely see each other.

I'm drawn to work that changes lives. Education, conscious business, impact finance, social enterprise. If the work matters, I'm interested. If it's just money moving around, I'm not your guy.

I hold a deep spiritual view of life. I won't preach it at you, but it shapes everything: how I treat people, why I choose the work I choose, and my conviction that a life lived from your own truth beats one lived from anyone else's script.

Beyond Work

The rest of the picture

The Running Man

For a while I travelled the world filming one dance in as many places as I could get to. It got picked up by international media and ended with me dancing in front of roughly half a million people at a kite festival in Colombo. Life is meant to be fun and interesting.

Places I've Called Home

Two years in China, split between Shanghai and Beijing. Six months in Dubai. Twelve months in Tbilisi, Georgia, from January 2022, arriving at the tail end of covid and just before the Russia-Ukraine conflict began. An interesting time to be there.

Chiang Mai

Home base is northern Thailand: mountain air, good coffee, a scooter and a suitcase never fully unpacked. The best decisions I make happen away from the noise.

People
