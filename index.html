Hi, my name's Robbie.

I'm an adventurous soul who's currently busy remembering who I am.

A little life update, a few thoughts about consciousness, and a reminder to have fun along the way.

In this noisy world, it's easy to find ourselves walking down paths that aren't aligned with our values, our truth or our inner knowing.

But we all have an Inner Guidance System, and mine has been telling me for a while now:

“Hey, wrong way mate!”

I eventually listened.

Right now, I'm exploring what a truly authentic and Soul-Centred life might look like for me.

Once I know and remember, I'll come back here and update this site.

But in the meantime, I want to share something with you…

We live in a world that's fascinated, perhaps even obsessed, with technological advancement.

I love technology as much as the next person. In fact, I've been using AI and large language models almost every day for nearly four years.

As humanity, we also seem rather obsessed with accumulating as much wealth, money, success and as many things as possible.

And there's nothing inherently wrong with any of that!

Hey, stuff and things can be awesome!

But when we develop an unhealthy relationship with acquiring and holding on to them, that's when things can become problematic.

In our individual and collective pursuit of having more of everything, I've noticed there's something crucial, something deeply important, that we've largely forgotten along the way.

What is it?

CONSCIOUSNESS

Yes, that's right.

Consciousness.

Technology, money, success, growth, you name it. All of these things can be fabulous and amazing for humanity.

But imagine what becomes possible when the Advancement of Human Consciousness progresses alongside them.

When our technological and material advancements are guided by greater awareness, compassion, wisdom and love, they can genuinely benefit us all.

Unfortunately, that's not the world we're seeing right now.

We have enormous concentrations of wealth and power, while the gap between the haves and the have-nots continues to widen.

So, why am I sharing all of this?

Because I want to play my part in advancing human consciousness.

And that's why I've decided to stop walking down a path that no longer feels right for me.

The question that matters most to me now is:

How can I play my part in advancing human consciousness?

At the moment, I'm not entirely sure what that looks like.

And that's okay.

When I have a clearer picture, I'll return and share it right here.

In the meantime, please shine your light as brightly as you can.

Even the tiniest light can illuminate the darkness.

We are all Divinity having a human experience.

Most of us have simply forgotten that for a while.

But more and more of us are waking up from the dream of humanity.

One by one, our lights will shine, and together, Heaven on Earth can become our collective reality.

Until then, I send you my love and good vibes.

Remember who you are.

Remember why you came.

And most importantly…

Remember to have fun!
